🌧️ Ek Khoobsurat Yaad...

Jo Shayad Tum Bhool Gayi... Lekin Main Kabhi Nahi Bhool Paaya. ❤️

Pata nahi tumhe yaad hai ya nahi... Lekin mujhe aaj bhi woh baarish wala din bilkul waise hi yaad hai, jaise kal ki baat ho. 🌧️ Us din baarish ho rahi thi... aur hum dono bas kuch der, shayad 4–5 minute ke liye ek hi raaste par saath chale the. Us chhote se safar mein pehli baar itni der tak tumse face to face baat karne ka mauka mila tha. Shayad tumhare liye woh bas ek aam sa din raha hoga... Lekin mere liye... Woh meri zindagi ki ab tak ki sabse khoobsurat aur sabse pyari memory hai. ❤️ Aaj bhi jab kabhi baarish hoti hai... To bina kisi wajah ke wahi din yaad aa jata hai. Aur pata hai... Us yaad ke saath chehre par ek muskurahat apne aap aa jaati hai. 🌸 Ho sakta hai tumhe ye sab yaad bhi na ho... Lekin kuch lamhe sirf kuch minute ke hote hain, Aur phir bhi poori zindagi ke liye yaad ban jaate hain. Mere liye woh 4–5 minute bhi unhi lamhon mein se ek hain. Thank You... Meri zindagi ki itni khoobsurat yaadon ka hissa banne ke liye. 🤍 Aur aaj... Tumhare birthday par bas itni si dua hai... Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe, Tumhari har dua qubool kare, Aur tumhari zindagi hamesha muskurahaton se bhari rahe. ❤️ Happy Birthday Once Again, a. ❤️
